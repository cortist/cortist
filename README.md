## Evidence over hype

Cortist is an independent AI evidence and forecasting project. We follow consequential developments in artificial intelligence, preserve original sources, and examine the difference between **what was promised**, **what the evidence shows**, and **what remains unresolved**.

Our coverage spans **AGI, superintelligence, AI safety, AI agents, jobs, medicine, and energy**.

## Public evidence data

The Reality Ledger includes dated, machine-readable records and source references.

- [Browse the public JSON index](https://cortist.com/data/reality-ledger/index.json)
- [Explore the evidence methodology](https://cortist.com/methodology/)
- [Read the editorial standards](https://cortist.com/editorial-standards/)

**How to interpret these records:** An announcement is not the same as an independently verified outcome. Open questions remain open until relevant supporting evidence is available. Historical snapshots should remain traceable when assessments are updated.

> Public access to Cortist data or links to third-party research does not automatically grant a license to redistribute underlying third-party materials or Cortist editorial content. Check applicable rights before reuse.
